Saathi

A clickable prototype of a student wellbeing app, shaped by 41 responses to the ING student wellbeing survey. For now, all data stays in this browser.

  • Anonymous by defaultFear of judgment (17) and privacy (11) were top reasons students avoid help.
  • Daily check-in and two-week screening, from the Today screen37% scored moderate or severe on the PHQ-4 items; 16 weren't sure their problem was "serious enough".
  • Reminders that ringCheck-in, meditation, study and bedtime reminders, as phone notifications.
  • Tools for coping alone19 prefer to handle things themselves; concentration trouble affected 53%.
  • Body tabTiredness (51%), sleep (38%) and appetite (33%) problems, plus the printable emergency card from EduCare. Its QR works offline.
  • Support tab14 didn't know where to go and 17 said help costs too much, so free lines come first.
